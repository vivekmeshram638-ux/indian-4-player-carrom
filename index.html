Paneer Pulao

delicious and nutritious Indian dish made with rice and paneer. It includes basmati rice, soft paneer cubes, and vegetables like peas, carrots, and beans.
To enhance its flavor, spices such as cumin seeds, bay leaf, cloves, and garam masala are added. Paneer Pulao is easy to prepare and is a perfect option for lunch or dinner.
It has a mild spicy taste and is loved by everyone.

    Ingredients

  • Basmati rice
  • Paneer (cottage cheese)
  • Peas
  • Onion
  • Spices
  • Oil
  • Salt
  • Water

    Steps

  1. Wash the rice
  2. Heat oil
  3. Add spices
  4. Add rice and water
  5. Cook well
